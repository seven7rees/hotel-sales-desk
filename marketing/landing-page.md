## Subhead
Claude-powered workflows for BEOs, group resumes, contracts, and inbox triage, built for hotels and resorts running Amadeus Delphi on Salesforce.

## The problem
Your team spends hours a week on repetitive Delphi work: patching item types that the API silently drops, chasing $0 line items, rebuilding resumes by hand, checking whether a contract merge field pulled stale revenue. None of it is hard. All of it eats the time your DOSM and catering managers should be spending on clients.

## What it does
- **BEOs.** Build, fix, and generate Banquet Event Orders. Catches the item-type and pricing bugs that cause $0 lines and missing quantities before they print.
- **Group resumes.** Produces the rooms and logistics resume straight from Delphi, fills what the template leaves blank.
- **Contracts and addenda.** Generates from your own Delphi templates, checks the revenue math against stale merge fields before anything goes to a client.
- **Inbox triage.** Sorts a day of sales mail into action buckets and drafts on-brand replies, in your font, with your signature.

## How it works
1. We connect it to your Salesforce org (read-only to start).
2. A short setup call fills in your property's numbers: function space, financials, templates, signature.
3. Your team runs it inside Claude Code. Every draft is reviewed by a human before it sends. Nothing signs, sends, or commits pricing on its own.

## Built by someone who runs a desk
Not a vendor demo. Built and used inside a working hotel sales and events department on live Delphi data, then generalized so no property's data ships with it.
