### Notes
- First 10 to 15 sends: properties you already have a relationship with (industry contacts, past colleagues, Delphi user groups). Warm beats cold here.
- Don't attach the repo. Offer the live demo instead; the code is the product, not the pitch.
- Track sends and replies in a simple sheet: property, contact, date sent, reply, next step.
