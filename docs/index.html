Your sales and events team is still doing Delphi by hand. It doesn't have to be.

Claude-powered workflows for BEOs, group resumes, contracts, and inbox triage — built for hotels and resorts running Amadeus Delphi on Salesforce.

The problem

Your team spends hours a week on repetitive Delphi work: patching item types the API silently drops, chasing $0 line items, rebuilding resumes by hand, checking whether a contract merge field pulled stale revenue. None of it is hard. All of it eats the time your DOSM and catering managers should be spending on clients.

What it does

SkillReplaces
delphi-beoBuilding, fixing, and generating Banquet Event Orders; catches $0-line and missing-quantity bugs before they print.
delphi-group-resumeProducing the rooms/logistics resume straight from Delphi, filling what the template leaves blank.
delphi-contractsGenerating contracts and addenda from your own Delphi templates, checking the revenue math against stale merge fields.
sales-inbox-triageSorting a day of sales mail into action buckets and drafting on-brand replies.

How it works

  • Connects to your Salesforce org, read-only to start.
  • A short setup session fills in your property's numbers: function space, financials, templates, signature.
  • Your team runs it inside Claude Code. Every draft is reviewed by a human before it sends. Nothing signs, sends, or commits pricing on its own.
